Family Vacation: Yellowstone National Park

Overview

The Carter family’s trip to Yellowstone National Park was one of their most memorable adventures. They spent a week exploring the park’s natural wonders, wildlife, and scenic landscapes.

Activities

  • Hiking scenic trails around geysers and waterfalls
  • Watching geothermal features like Old Faithful erupt
  • Wildlife spotting tours at sunrise and sunset
  • Family picnics near Yellowstone Lake
  • Photography of landscapes and animals

What They Saw

  • Geysers, including the famous Old Faithful
  • Hot springs with vibrant colors
  • Waterfalls such as the Lower Falls of the Yellowstone River
  • Wildlife: bison, elk, deer, and even a distant grizzly bear
  • Expansive forests and mountain views

Favorite Parts of the Trip

  • James: Watching Old Faithful erupt and exploring the park’s geology
  • Emily: Peaceful mornings by Yellowstone Lake and wildlife sightings
  • Olivia: Taking photos of the colorful hot springs and sunsets
  • Liam: Spotting bison up close and hiking with the family
  • Sophia: Seeing waterfalls and enjoying family picnics

Memorable Moment

One of the most unforgettable moments was when the family watched a herd of bison slowly cross the road just a few feet from their car — a true Yellowstone experience!
